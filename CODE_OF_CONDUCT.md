
# 🛰️ Code of Conduct — THE MRIDANSH HQ

## Our Commitment

THE MRIDANSH HQ (Jagannath Command Center) is built and maintained with the same rigor expected of mission-critical systems. We extend that same standard to how contributors and users treat each other. We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or affiliation.

---

## 🧭 Expected Behavior

- Communicate with respect, clarity, and precision — the same discipline expected of the codebase.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before assuming malice or incompetence.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and approaches to problem-solving.
- Be considerate of maintainers' and contributors' time.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments — public or private
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or bad-faith reporting
- Sustained disruption of discussions, Issues, or Pull Requests
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional engineering environment

---

## 🛡️ Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, commit messages, and any official communication channels — as well as in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

---

## 🚨 Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainer through the repository's listed contact method. Reports will be:

- Reviewed promptly and confidentially
- Handled with respect for the reporter's privacy
- Followed by appropriate corrective action, at the maintainer's discretion

For security vulnerabilities specifically, follow the process in `SECURITY.md` instead.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any instance of unacceptable conduct. This may include:

1. **Warning** — a private, written warning with clarity on the violation
2. **Temporary restriction** — temporary ban from interaction within project spaces
3. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations

---
